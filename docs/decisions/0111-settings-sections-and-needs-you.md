# 0111: Settings by what's in them, and Needs you in the top bar

Date: 2026-11-07

## Decision

Settings stay one sheet with its menu (ADR 0100). In a section, fields sit in titled groups on a six-column grid, each as wide as what it holds, in place of ADR 0106's rows that stretched every field across the page.

- Opening hours are a switch per day. An open day is a white row with its times, a closed one falls back into the sheet. The time zone is here, as it's what these hours are read in.
- Tickets is one switch and a number picked with a slider, a stepper or presets, beside a calendar of the days it counts and the date it leads to. A number outside 1 to 60 can't be chosen any more, so the form has no validation of its own.
- Google reviews shows what Customers get instead of a guide and a link to the same guide. Checking the link is ADR 0109.
- Delete is a danger zone: what's lost, in red, then a dialog that says what's still open and asks for the shop's name.

Needs you leaves Today for a bell in the top bar, on every page of a shop, with a solid count and a wide list. Coming up is gone. With nothing beside it, the table takes the page's whole width, under the stats and the week's chart.

## Why

A short postal code in a field as wide as the page read as unfinished, and a turnaround typed as a number said nothing about the date it promises.

What needs someone doesn't stop mattering on another page, and a tile on Today capped how much of it could show. Coming up only showed avatars and a count, which no one could act on.
