# 0115: The pricing panel

Date: 2026-11-07

## Decision

The plan chooser is a panel of its own, the whole screen on a phone. Pro is the page's one loud thing, a solid pine tile with a very large price, because it's what a shop has been using during its trial. Essentiel sits beside it, quiet. Each plan has its own button, so one tap leads to payment.

With a trial's days left, Pro draws what happens: nothing to pay today, then the price on the day it's charged. Yearly shows the monthly equivalent, what's paid once a year, and what's saved. Essentiel says plainly what it leaves out.

It stays on monthly until someone chooses yearly, so nobody reaches €490 by accident.

## Why

The old chooser was a list of two radio buttons and a Continue button in a small dialog: two steps, and nothing making the case. What holds a small shop back is paying before being sure, so the panel answers that first.

Nothing here is invented to hurry anyone. No countdown, and no "most popular", as there are no customers to say so. Each claim is true of this shop.
