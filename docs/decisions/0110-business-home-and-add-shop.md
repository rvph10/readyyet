# 0110: What a Business's home shows, and adding a shop

Date: 2026-11-07

## Decision

A shop's tile on the Business's home shows what decides if it's worth opening: its open Tickets as one big number, a bar of how many are ready, late or in progress, and those two counts. Its plan only shows when it needs a look, a plan in good order is on Billing. This narrows ADR 0092's tile and keeps ADR 0105's rule that the home is its Locations.

Adding a shop is a drawer with three steps, name, trade and contact, opened from anywhere an Owner has the button. It replaces ADR 0102's page, and the drawer loads what it needs when it opens. The last tile of the home is the same action.

The invitation to another shop says what each side gets and offers to copy, email or send the link on WhatsApp. The address isn't shown.

## Why

The Owner asked if data belongs here at all. It does when it tells them where to go, and not otherwise: the rest is on the shop's Today.

The form's preview needed half a page, and adding a shop is three short questions.
