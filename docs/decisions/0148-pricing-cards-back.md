# 0148: The home's pricing cards are back

Date: 2026-11-12

Replaces what was left of ADR 0146: the Nexio switch on the home.

## Decision

The home's pricing is Contelas' two cards again (ADR 0133), as they were before ADR 0146: Essentiel in grey, Pro in pine with its "Popular" tag, each price followed by its yearly one. Nexio's files are removed.

## Why

The user preferred the cards they had. The yearly price was already on each card, so the switch added motion rather than information.
