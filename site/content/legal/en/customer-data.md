# Your data as a customer of a shop using ReadyYet

You are reading this because a shop is working on your item (a repair, a cleaning, an alteration…) and uses ReadyYet to keep you informed.

## Who is responsible

**The shop** decides to record your details and is responsible for them (the "controller"). Its name and contact details are on your tracking page and in every email about your item.

**ReadyYet** stores and sends this information for the shop and only on its instructions (a "processor"). ReadyYet is run by Raphaël Genu, self-employed, trading as UpInTown, Kerkedelle 51A, 1200 Woluwe-Saint-Lambert, Belgium, enterprise number 1034.448.184.

## What is recorded

- Your name, and the email address and phone number you gave the shop.
- The item and the work: what the shop wrote about it, its status and dates, when it should be ready, when you collected it, and photos the shop took of it (which may show a person or a number plate).
- If you answer the shop's request for feedback: your rating, the tags you picked and your message, and the shop's reply.
- The emails sent to you about your item and whether they were delivered.

ReadyYet does not use any of this for its own purposes, does not sell it, and does not run analytics or advertising cookies on your tracking page.

## The emails you receive

The shop sends you emails through ReadyYet when your item is registered, when its status changes, when a ready date is set or approaches, and, if the shop turned it on, a request for feedback once the work is done.

**Every email has a "Stop updates" link.** It stops all further emails about that item at once.

## How long it is kept

- Photos: deleted 30 days after the work on the item ended.
- The content of emails: deleted after 30 days.
- Everything else: deleted 24 months after your last item at that shop was closed, or earlier if the shop deletes it or closes its account.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to the emails. **Ask the shop**, using the contact details on your tracking page: it can do this directly in ReadyYet. If you write to ReadyYet instead (privacy@readyyet.app), we will pass your request on to the shop.

You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority: Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, www.autoriteprotectiondonnees.be.

## Where the data is

In the European Union: hosted in Amsterdam (the Netherlands), emails sent from Ireland, error reports in Germany, all stripped of your details where possible. The providers are listed in our [Terms of Service, Annex 3](terms#annex-3).
