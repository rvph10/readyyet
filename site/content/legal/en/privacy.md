## 3. How long we keep it

- **Account data**: as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your name and email are replaced by "Former member" and an address that receives nothing; your sessions and memberships are deleted. What you did in a shop's workspace stays attached to "Former member".
- **Sessions and sign-in codes**: deleted when they expire (a session lasts 7 days and renews while you use the service).
- **Emails we send you**: their content is kept 30 days for delivery and support, then only the record that an email was sent is kept.
- **Billing records**: 10 years, as Belgian tax law requires (accounting law alone requires 7).
- **Analytics**: [TO SET: the shortest period PostHog's plan allows, e.g. 12 months].
- **Messages to support**: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

**Only with your consent:**

| Name           | Set by                         | What for                                                                          | Lasts  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| `ph_…_posthog` | readyyet.app, app.readyyet.app | Analytics: recognise your device across visits to measure how the service is used | 1 year |

Until you accept, no analytics run at all: nothing is stored on your device and nothing is sent to PostHog. The banner offers "Accept" and "Refuse" side by side; refusing has no effect on the service. You can change your choice at any time, in one click, from the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page; withdrawing deletes the analytics cookies. We ask again after 6 months. Tracking pages for shops' customers (`/t/…`) never run analytics.

This section also covers similar techniques such as local storage. Legal basis: article 10/2 of the Belgian law of 30 July 2018 and GDPR art. 6(1)(a). This cookie policy is versioned; earlier versions are kept.

## 6. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before. You can delete your account yourself from your profile. Write to privacy@readyyet.app; we answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority: Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, www.autoriteprotectiondonnees.be.

## 7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to production is limited to the publisher, database backups are kept up to 27 days, and error reports are stripped of personal data before they leave the service.

We will announce material changes by email to Owners and on this page at least 30 days before they apply.
