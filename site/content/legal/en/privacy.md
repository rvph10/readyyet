## 3. How long we keep it

- **Account data**: as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your name is replaced by "Former member" and your email by an address that receives nothing; your sessions and memberships are deleted. What you did in a shop's workspace stays attached to "Former member". If you are the Owner of an active Business, transfer it or delete its Locations first.
- **Sessions and sign-in codes**: deleted within a day after they expire (a session lasts 7 days and renews while you use the service).
- **Invitations**: deleted 30 days after their expiry date, whether accepted or not.
- **Emails we send you**: their content is kept 30 days for delivery and support. The record of an email (recipient, type, dates and delivery status) is kept 24 months.
- **Acceptance of the terms**: for the length of the contract and 10 years after.
- **Billing records and sales partner commissions**: 10 years, as Belgian tax law requires.
- **Analytics**: 1 year, then PostHog deletes them.
- **Messages to support**: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

We use as few cookies as possible.

**Always on, no consent needed** (strictly necessary or set at your request):

**Only with your consent:**

| Name           | Set by                         | What for                                                                          | Lasts  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| `ph_…_posthog` | readyyet.app, app.readyyet.app | Analytics: recognise your device across visits to measure how the service is used | 1 year |

Until you accept, nothing is measured: nothing is stored on your device and nothing is sent to PostHog. The banner offers "Accept" and "Refuse" side by side; refusing has no effect on the service. You can change your choice at any time, in one click, from the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page of readyyet.app and of the sign-in page, or in your profile menu once signed in; withdrawing deletes the analytics cookies. We ask again after 6 months. Tracking pages for shops' customers (`/t/…`) never measure anything.

This section also covers similar techniques such as local storage. Legal basis: article 10/2 of the Belgian law of 30 July 2018 and GDPR art. 6(1)(a). This cookie policy is versioned; earlier versions are kept.

## 6. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, delete or export your data, and to restrict how we use it. You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

**You can object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interest.**

You can delete your account yourself from your profile. Write to privacy@readyyet.app; we answer within one month, which the law allows us to extend by two months for complex requests.

You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority: Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, www.autoriteprotectiondonnees.be.

## 7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access to production is limited to the publisher, every database backup is deleted within 27 days, and error reports are stripped of personal data before they leave the service.
